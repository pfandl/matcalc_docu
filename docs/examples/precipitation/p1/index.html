Example P1: Precipitation of cementite in Fe-0.1%C

This example shows the basic setup of how to simulate a precipitation kinetic simulation using MatCalc. All basic features and options will be explained in the first part, while the second part focuses on improving the quality of the results of part one. The obtained results will be compared to the LSW-Theory.
