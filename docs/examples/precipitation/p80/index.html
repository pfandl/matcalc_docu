Example P80: Precipitation of coherent Cu in Fe-Cu

Advanced simulation of copper precipitation in Fe-Cu binary system. Part 1 of this example will elaborate on the models, which are required for a predictive simulation of the problem. Part 2 will demonstrate the precipitation simulation on the basis of the experimental values from Goodman et al., Metall. Trans. 4 (1973) 2363-2369.
