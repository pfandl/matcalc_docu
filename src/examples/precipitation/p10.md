Example P10: AlN precipitation in steel
---------------------------------------

This example provides a set of *MatCalc* scripts for simulation of AlN precipitation in steel. This study was published in ref.[1)](#fn__1) The main feature of these examples is the simultaneous precipitation of AlN on dislocations and grain boundaries. The simulations make use of volumetric misfit data for AlN in the nucleation stage as well as the special diffusion geometry for grain boundary precipitation developed in ref.[2)](#fn__2)

* *P10-1* -  [AlN precipitation in austenite](/examples/precipitation/p10/p10_1 "examples/precipitation/p10/p10_1")
* *P10-2* -  [AlN precipitation in ferrite](/examples/precipitation/p10/p10_2 "examples/precipitation/p10/p10_2")

[1)](#fnt__1)

R. Radis and E. Kozeschnik, “Kinetics of AlN precipitation in microalloyed steel”, Model. Simul. Mater. Sci. Eng. 18 (2010) 055003 (16pp)

[2)](#fnt__2)

E. Kozeschnik, J. Svoboda, R. Radis and F.D. Fischer, “Mean-field model for the growth and coarsening of stoichiometric precipitates at grain boundaries”, Model. Simul. Mater. Sci. Eng. 18 (2010) 015011 (19pp)
