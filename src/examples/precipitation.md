Precipitation kinetics
----------------------

The precipitation kinetics module of MatCalc provides a powerful means of evaluating the evolution of precipitates during thermal and thermo-mechanical treatment.

* **P1** -  [Precipitation of cementite in Fe-0.1%C](/examples/precipitation/p1 "examples/precipitation/p1") - Evaluation of precipitation parameters, size distribution and asymptotic behavior. Comparison with LSW.

  + *P1-1* -  [A typical setup for a precipitation kinetics simulation](/examples/precipitation/p1/p1_1 "examples/precipitation/p1/p1_1")
  + *P1-2* -  [Setup of simulation for analysis of precipitate distributions](/examples/precipitation/p1/p1_2 "examples/precipitation/p1/p1_2")
* **P2** -  [Precipitation sequence in Al-Cu](/examples/precipitation/p2 "examples/precipitation/p2") - Isothermal and continuous heat treatment in Al-alloys considering FSK-model for vacancy evolution.

  + *P2-1* -  [Isothermal treatment of Al-Cu](/examples/precipitation/p2/p2_1 "examples/precipitation/p2/p2_1")
  + *P2-2* -  [Evolution of excess vacancies](/examples/precipitation/p2/p2_2 "examples/precipitation/p2/p2_2")
* **P5** -  [Concurrent growth of precipitates in Fe-12%Cr-0.1%C](/examples/precipitation/p5 "examples/precipitation/p5") - Evaluation of concurrent growth of M3C, M7C3 and M23C6. Evolution of precipitate Cr-content.

  + *P5-1* -  [Simulating growth of precipitates](/examples/precipitation/p5/p5_1 "examples/precipitation/p5/p5_1")
  + *P5-2* -  [Evaluation of inner particle diffusion coefficient](/examples/precipitation/p5/p5_2 "examples/precipitation/p5/p5_2")
* **P10** -  [AlN precipitation in steel](/examples/precipitation/p10 "examples/precipitation/p10") - Heterogeneous precipitation of AlN at dislocations and grain boundaries. Simulation and experimental verification.

  + *P10-1* -  [AlN precipitation in austenite](/examples/precipitation/p10/p10_1 "examples/precipitation/p10/p10_1")
  + *P10-2* -  [AlN precipitation in ferrite](/examples/precipitation/p10/p10_2 "examples/precipitation/p10/p10_2")
* **P12** -  [NbC precipitation in steel](/examples/precipitation/p12 "examples/precipitation/p12") - Interaction between precipitation and deformation
* **P13** -  [NbC and AlN precipitation in the deformed steel](/examples/precipitation/p13 "examples/precipitation/p13") - Interaction between precipitation and deformation.
* **P20** -  [Precipitation simulation during continuous casting of micro-alloyed Fe-Al-C-N-Nb-Ti steel](/examples/precipitation/p20 "examples/precipitation/p20") - Precipitation in primary solidification microstructure

  + *P20-1* -  [Treatment of primary precipitates](/examples/precipitation/p20/p20_1 "examples/precipitation/p20/p20_1")
  + *P20-2* -  [Precipitation kinetics in segregated and depleted regions](/examples/precipitation/p20/p20_2 "examples/precipitation/p20/p20_2")
* **P21** -  [Precipitation simulation during continuous casting of micro-alloyed Fe-Al-C-Cu-Mn-N-Nb-S-Si-Ti steel](/examples/precipitation/p21 "examples/precipitation/p21") - Simultaneous precipitation of two precipitates
* **P30** -  [Precipitation simulation in austenitic 316L steel](/examples/precipitation/p30 "examples/precipitation/p30")

  + *P30-1* -  [Evolution of Sigma, Chi and Laves precipitates](/examples/precipitation/p30/p30_1 "examples/precipitation/p30/p30_1")
* **P80** -  [Precipitation of coherent Cu in Fe-Cu](/examples/precipitation/p80 "examples/precipitation/p80") - Advanced simulation of bcc copper precipitation in bcc Fe.

  + *P80-1* -  [Models and parameters for Fe-Cu simulation](/examples/precipitation/p80/p80_1 "examples/precipitation/p80/p80_1")
  + *P80-2* -  [Precipitation of Cu in Fe-Cu](/examples/precipitation/p80/p80_2 "examples/precipitation/p80/p80_2")
  + *P80-3* -  [TTP plot in Fe-Cu system](/examples/precipitation/p80/p80_3 "examples/precipitation/p80/p80_3")
