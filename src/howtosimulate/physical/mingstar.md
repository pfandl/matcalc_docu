Calculation and visualization of minimum G\* energies
-----------------------------------------------------

* [Calculation of minimum G\* and nucleation probability P array](/howtosimulate/physical/mingstar/calc "howtosimulate/physical/mingstar/calc") - The calculation of min G\* and P-nucl values in form of an array is shown in the Fe-Cu alloy. Both values are given as a function of temperature and Cu. An alternative example on G\* and P calculation on VCN is elaborated.
* [Visualization of minimum G\* array](/howtosimulate/physical/mingstar/vis "howtosimulate/physical/mingstar/vis") - Using open source software GNUPlot to exemplify the visualization of calculated data. Possibilities on modifying axes and other option is shown.
