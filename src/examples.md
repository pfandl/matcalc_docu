MatCalc examples
================

The example documents of this sections are aimed at *introducing you to typical simulation problems* that can be solved with MatCalc. The examples demonstrate how these problems can be treated and which issues need to be accounted for to obtain reasonable simulation results.

If you are a newbie to MatCalc, you can benefit from this section because it also demonstrated the various simulation approaches that are implemented in MatCalc and how these are utilized to solve your problems. For practical use, we recommend, however, that you make yourself familiar also with the software using the  [tutorials](/matcalc_docu/tutorials "tutorials").

Typical examples of thermodynamic analysis, precipitation kinetics, long-range diffusion analysis, as well as Monte Carlo simulations are listed below.

Follow these links to specific examples for …

* [Equilibrium and non-equilibrium analysis](/matcalc_docu/examples/equilib "examples:equilib")
* [Precipitation kinetics](/matcalc_docu/examples/precipitation "examples:precipitation")
* [Thermo-physical property simulation](/matcalc_docu/examples/tprops "examples:tprops")
* [Long-range diffusion, and diffusion coupled with precipitation](/matcalc_docu/examples/diffusion "examples:diffusion")
* [Thermal simulations](/matcalc_docu/examples/therm_sims "examples:therm_sims")

Whereas the examples give instructions on how to perform your desired calculations with *MatCalc*, the  [How-to manual](/matcalc_docu/howtosimulate "howtosimulate") provides additional and complementary information.
